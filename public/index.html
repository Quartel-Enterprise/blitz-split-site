FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need to create an account?

No. The app starts with an anonymous id and gives you a recovery code to get back in on another device. Connecting Google or Apple is optional and only serves to sync and recover your access.

Does it really work offline?

Yes. Everything you do saves on the device right away. What hasn't reached the server yet waits in a queue and goes up as soon as you're back online.

Does the app move my money?

No. It keeps the group's accounts. Payments happen in your bank or wallet, and you record them in the app.

Who sees what I add?

Only the people in that group see its bills, payments and members. Your email stays out of it.

Can my friends use it on another phone?

Yes. Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac and the browser share the same groups, so everyone uses what they already have.
