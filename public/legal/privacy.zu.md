## Idatha esiyiphathayo {#data}

### Lokho osinika khona

- **Iphrofayela:** igama lakho, isithombe (asibophi) nombala we-avatar yakho.
- **Amaqembu nezindleko:** igama leqembu nohlobo lwemali yalo; incazelo yesindleko ngasinye, inani, usuku, isigaba, ubani okhokhile nokuthi sihlukaniswe kanjani; izinto, amazwana nezithombe ezinamathiselwe.
- **Izinkokhelo** ozirekhodayo phakathi kwamalungu.
- **Okhiye bokukhokha** obalondolozayo ukuze uthole imali: ukhiye we-Pix nekheli le-Bitcoin.
- **Izindlela zokukhokha** okhokha ngazo (ikhadi lesikweletu noma ledebhithi, i-Pix, ukudlulisela imali ebhange, i-Bitcoin, ukheshi noma enye): igama lazo, uhlobo nombala, hhayi inombolo yekhadi, nokuthi ukhokhe isindleko ngasinye ngayiphi.
- **Imali engenayo**, kuphela uma uhlukanisa “Ngokwemali engenayo”.
- **Abantu obengezayo** eqenjini ngaphambi kokuba babe nayo i-app, ngaphansi kwegama obanika lona.
- **Amazwana** owashiya esindlekweni othile ayabelana nawe ngaso ngelinki, ngaphansi kwegama lakho.
- **Imilayezo eya kusizo** oyithumela ku-Iphrofayela › “Xhumana nosizo”, nge-imeyili. I-app yengeza inguqulo yayo, inkundla, ulimi lwayo nohlobo lwe-akhawunti, hhayi i-ID ye-akhawunti yakho.

### I-akhawunti yakho

- **Ngaphandle kwe-akhawunti:** i-ID engaziwa, eyakhiwa lapho uvula i-app, nekhodi yokubuyisela ebuyisela ukufinyelela kwakho kwenye idivayisi.
- **Nge-Google noma i-Apple (akubophi):** i-imeyili ne-ID ye-akhawunti umhlinzeki asithumelela yona futhi, uma ukhetha, nesithombe. Nge-Apple ungayifihla i-imeyili yakho.

### Okuqoqwa ngokuzenzakalelayo

- **Izibalo zokusetshenziswa** (i-Google Firebase Analytics): izikrini ezivuliwe nezenzo ezenziwe, njengokuthi “wakha iqembu”, nedatha yedivayisi (imodeli, isistimu, ulimi, inguqulo ye-app nezwe cishe). Azilokothi ziphathe amagama, amanani noma ama-ID amaqembu, ezindleko noma abantu. Asisebenzisi i-ID yokukhangisa, futhi ukwenziwa kwezikhangiso ngokomuntu kuvaliwe. Uma unayo i-akhawunti, engaziwa noma ye-Google noma ye-Apple, izibalo ziphatha i-ID yayo, ikhodi engahleliwe engelona igama lakho noma i-imeyili yakho, ukuze sibone ukuthi i-app isetshenziswa kanjani efonini yakho, kukhompyutha nasesipheqululini. Uzivala kudivayisi ngayinye ku-Iphrofayela › Ubumfihlo nolwazi › “Yabelana ngedatha yokusetshenziswa”.
- **Imibiko yokuphahlazeka** (i-Firebase Crashlytics kuma-app e-Android, i-iPhone, i-iPad ne-Mac, ne-Sentry esipheqululini): uhlobo lwephutha nalapho lwenzeke khona kukhodi, nenguqulo ye-app nedatha yedivayisi noma yesiphequluli (imodeli, isistimu), ngaphandle kwemilayezo, engase iqukathe idatha yakho.
- **Ithokheni yezaziso:** ikheli i-Google, i-Apple noma isiphequluli esinika lona idivayisi ukuze ithole izaziso.
- **Idatha yokuxhumana**, njenge-IP address, eqoshwa abahlinzeki bengqalasizinda yethu.

I-app **isebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kuqala**: lokho okwengezayo kulondolozwa kusizindalwazi esikudivayisi uqobo (esipheqululini, ngaphakathi kwesitoreji sesayithi). Uma kunokuxhumana, idatha iya kuseva yethu, evumelanisa amadivayisi akho futhi inike ilungu ngalinye okweqembu.

> **Skena irisidi**
>
> Isithombe siya kuseva yethu futhi sifundwa yimodeli ye-AI ye-Anthropic (Claude), ebuyisa izinto, amanani nesamba. Asisigcini isithombe ngemva kokusifunda: sihlala esindlekweni kuphela uma usinamathisela. Ngaphansi kwemigomo yezentengiselwano ye-Anthropic, lokhu okuqukethwe akusetshenziselwa ukuqeqesha amamodeli.

> **Mema kusuka koxhumana nabo**

## Obani ababona idatha yakho {#sees}

### Abantu abaseqenjini lakho

Noma ubani oseqenjini ubona igama lelungu ngalinye, isithombe nombala; izindleko zaleso qembu, izinto, amazwana, izithombe nezinkokhelo; nokhiye bokukhokha ilungu elibabonisayo ukuze likhokhelwe. **Akekho eqenjini obona i-imeyili yakho**, noma izindlela zakho zokukhokha: nguwe kuphela ozibonayo, kumadivayisi akho.

### Obani abanelinki oyabelana ngayo

- **Yabelana ngesindleko** kwenza ilinki yesindleko esisodwa. Ngokuthi “Abantu abasesindlekweni kuphela”, abantu abakuso kuphela abasivulayo. Ngokuthi “Noma ubani onelinki”, noma ubani onelinki ubona isindleko (incazelo yaso, inani, usuku, isigaba negama leqembu, ubani okhokhile nokuthi sihlukaniswe kanjani, ngamagama) nezithombe zaso namazwana, futhi noma ubani ongene ngemvume ku-app angaphawula ngaso. Umbukiso wokuqala i-app yokuxoxa eyidweba kulinki ubonisa incazelo, inani, ubani okhokhile, sihlukaniswe phakathi kwabantu abangaki negama leqembu. Ukuthi “Vala ilinki”, ku-Yabelana ngesindleko, kuyayimisa ilinki ukuthi isebenze; umbukiso wokuqala i-app yokuxoxa esiwudwebile uhlala kuleyo ngxoxo, futhi isithombe sombukiso singagcinwa okwesikhashana kuze kube usuku olulodwa.
- **Yabelana ngephrofayela** kwenza ilinki yephrofayela yakho: noma ubani onayo ubona igama lakho nesithombe, nasembukisweni wokuqala welinki.

## Siyigcina isikhathi esingakanani {#keep}

- Uma nje i-akhawunti yakho isekhona, sigcina lokho i-app ekudingayo ukuze isebenze.
- **Phuma ngemvume** kusula ikhophi esedivayisini. Nge-Google noma i-Apple, idatha yakho ihlala kuseva ukuze uyithole lapho ungena ngemvume futhi.
- **Ngaphandle kwe-akhawunti**, ukuthi “Sula iphrofayela uphume ngemvume” kuhlanza idivayisi futhi ikhodi yakho yokubuyisela iqhubeka isebenza kuze kube yilapho i-akhawunti isuswa ngenxa yokungasetshenziswa (ngezansi). Ukuze usule ngokushesha lokho okuseseva, sebenzisa ukuthi “Susa i-akhawunti nedatha”.
- **I-akhawunti engenayo i-Google noma i-Apple iyasuswa ngemva kwezinsuku ezingu-30 ingasetshenziswa:** lapho ingekho neyodwa yamadivayisi ayo evumelanisa izinsuku ezingu-30, iseva iyayisusa njengoba kwenza ukuthi “Susa i-akhawunti nedatha”, futhi ikhodi yayo yokubuyisela iyayeka ukusebenza.
- **Ukuthi “Susa i-akhawunti nedatha”**, ku-Iphrofayela, kususa ngokushesha iphrofayela yakho, isithombe, okhiye bokukhokha, izindlela zokukhokha nekhodi yokubuyisela, futhi kuhoxisa ukufinyelela i-Apple ebikunikile. Izindleko owazengeza emaqenjini nabanye abantu zihlala lapho ukuze zisebenze eqenjini, ngaphansi kwegama obunalo kulo, zingasaxhumene nanoma iyiphi i-akhawunti. Kunjalo namazwana owashiya, nasezindlekweni ozabelwe ngelinki.
- Ama-imeyili osizo agcinwa uma nje siwadinga ukuze sikuphendule futhi silandelele lokho okubikile. Sicele futhi siyawasusa.
- Idatha yokusetshenziswa exhunywe nedivayisi ihlala ku-Google Analytics kuze kube izinyanga ezingu-14. Ngemva kwalokho, kusala izinombolo eziqoqiwe kuphela, kungekho lutho oluhlonza muntu. Uma i-akhawunti yakho isisusiwe, i-ID ezinayo ayisaholeli kuwena.
- Imibiko yokuphahlazeka igcinwa kuze kube izinsuku ezingu-90 bese iyasuswa.

Idatha ihamba ibethelwe (HTTPS). Kuseva, imithetho yokufinyelela iqinisekisa ukuthi umuntu ngamunye ufunda kuphela amaqembu angakuwo nalokho okuvulwa yilinki ayinikiwe. Ayikho isistimu engenaphutha, kodwa sithatha noma isiphi isehlakalo ngokungathi sína futhi sikwazise wena ne-ANPD lapho umthetho ukucela.

Ikhodi yakho yokubuyisela, amagama angu-12, isebenza njengephasiwedi: yigcine endaweni ephephile futhi ungabelani ngayo. Asiwagcini amagama, sigcina kuphela isigxivizo sawo esingenakubuyiselwa kuwo. Ku-iPhone, i-iPad ne-Mac, i-app iyilondoloza ku-app ethi Passwords.
