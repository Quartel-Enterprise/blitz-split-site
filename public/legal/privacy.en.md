> **In short**
>
> - You use the app without creating an account. Google and Apple are optional.
> - What you add stays on your device first, and only goes to the server to sync and reach your group.
> - People in a group see what belongs to the group. Nobody sees your email.
> - Usage metrics and crash reports carry no names or amounts. With an account, the metrics are tied to its id. We don't use an advertising id, and you turn the metrics off in Profile.
> - We don't sell data. You delete your account and data from the app itself.

## Who we are {#who}

This policy explains how **Blitz Split** (Bora Rachar in Brazil) handles personal data in the apps for Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac and the browser, and on the blitzsplit.com website. It is developed by **PI Software LTDA** (Quartel Enterprise), the data controller (“we”).

It follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018, the LGPD). By using the app you agree to the [Terms of use](/terms), which this policy is part of.

## The data we handle {#data}

### What you give us

- **Profile:** your name, a photo (optional) and your avatar color.
- **Groups and bills:** the group's name and currency; each bill's description, amount, date, category, who paid and how it was split; items, comments and attached photos.
- **Payments** you record between members.
- **Payment keys** you save: a Pix key and a Bitcoin address.
- **Incomes**, only if you split “By income”.
- **People you add** to a group before they have the app, under the name you give them.

### Your account

- **Without an account:** an anonymous id, created when you open the app, and a recovery code that restores your access on another device.
- **With Google or Apple (optional):** the email and account id the provider sends us and, if you choose, the photo. With Apple you can hide your email.

### What is collected automatically

- **Usage metrics** (Google Firebase Analytics): the screens opened and the actions taken, such as “created a group”, with device data (model, system, language, app version and approximate country). They never carry names, amounts or the ids of groups, bills or people. We don't use the advertising id, and ad personalization is off. If you have an account, anonymous or with Google or Apple, the metrics carry its id, a random code that isn't your name or email, so we see how the app is used across your phone, computer and browser. You turn them off on each device in Profile › Privacy and about › “Share usage data”.
- **Crash reports** (Firebase Crashlytics, in the Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac apps): the kind of error and where in the code it happened, without the messages, which could hold your data.
- **Notification token:** the address Google, Apple or the browser gives the device to receive notices.
- **Connection data**, such as the IP address, logged by our infrastructure providers.

### On the website

blitzsplit.com uses Google Analytics to count visits, in a project apart from the app's. Website visits never mix with how you use the app.

## Where your data lives {#where}

The app is **offline first**: what you add saves in a database on the device itself (in the browser, inside the site's storage). With a connection, the data goes to our server, which syncs your devices and gives each member what belongs to the group.

> **Scan receipt**
>
> The photo goes to our server and is read by an AI model from Anthropic (Claude), which returns the items, prices and total. We don't keep the photo after reading it: it only stays on the bill if you attach it. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this content isn't used to train models.

## Who sees your data {#sees}

### The people in your group

Anyone in a group sees each member's name, photo and color; that group's bills, items, comments, photos and payments; and the payment keys a member shows to get paid. **Nobody in the group sees your email.**

## Transfers to other countries {#abroad}

Some of these providers store or process data outside Brazil, mostly in the United States. These transfers follow article 33 of the LGPD, with the contractual clauses and safeguards each provider offers.

## How long we keep it {#keep}

- While your account exists, we keep what the app needs to work.
- **Sign out** erases the device's copy. With Google or Apple, your data stays on the server for when you sign in again.
- **Without an account**, “Erase profile and sign out” clears the device and your recovery code keeps working. To erase what is on the server too, contact us (section 12).
- **“Delete account and data”**, in Profile, deletes your profile, photo, payment keys and recovery code at once, and revokes the access Apple granted. Bills you added to groups with other people stay there for the group, without your name, as “Former member”.
- Usage data tied to a device stays in Google Analytics for up to 14 months. After that, only aggregated numbers remain, with nothing that identifies anyone. Once your account is deleted, the id they carry no longer leads to you.

## Your rights {#rights}

Under the LGPD (art. 18), you can:

- confirm whether we handle your data and access it;
- correct incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
- ask for unnecessary data to be anonymized, blocked or deleted;
- take your data to another service (portability);
- know who we share your data with;
- withdraw consent and object to processing based on legitimate interest;
- complain to Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

You do much of this in the app itself: edit your profile, delete a payment key, delete your account. For the rest, write to [quare.software@gmail.com](mailto:quare.software@gmail.com). We answer within 15 days.

## Security {#security}

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). On the server, access rules make sure each person only reads the groups they belong to. No system is flawless, but we take any incident seriously and notify you and the ANPD when the law asks.

Your recovery code works like a password: keep it somewhere safe and don't share it. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, the app saves it in the Passwords app.

## Children {#children}

Blitz Split isn't made for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data. If you learn of a case, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Changes to this policy {#changes}

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top. If the change matters, we tell you in the app before it takes effect.
