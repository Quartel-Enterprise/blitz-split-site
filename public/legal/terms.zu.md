## Ukwamukela {#accept}

Le migomo iphakathi kwakho ne-**PI Software LTDA** (Quartel Enterprise, “thina”) ngokusebenzisa ama-app e-Blitz Split (ebizwa ngokuthi yi-Bora Rachar eBrazil) e-Android, i-iPhone, i-iPad, i-Mac nesiphequluli, newebhusayithi i-blitzsplit.com.

Ngokusebenzisa i-app, uyavumelana nale migomo kanye ne-[Nqubomgomo yobumfihlo](/privacy). Uma ungavumi, ungayisebenzisi i-app.

## Okwenziwa yi-app {#service}

I-Blitz Split isiza amaqembu ukuthi abelane ngezindleko: irekhoda izindleko, ibala ukuthi ubani kufanele akhokhe bani malini, futhi igcina izinkokhelo ozirekhodayo.

> **Akulona ibhange noma isikhungo sezinkokhelo.**
>
> Ayikho imali edlula ku-app. I-Pix, i-Bitcoin noma enye indlela kwenzeka ngaphandle kwayo, phakathi kwakho namabhange akho noma izikhwama zakho. I-app ibonisa kuphela ukhiye noma ikheli ilungu ngalinye elikulondolozile.

Amabhalansi ayimiphumela yalokho iqembu elikwengezile. Akuyona i-invoyisi, ithuluzi lesikweletu noma ubufakazi besikweletu.

## I-akhawunti yakho nokufinyelela {#account}

- Ungasebenzisa i-app ngaphandle kwe-akhawunti. Ukuxhuma i-Google noma i-Apple akubophi futhi kusiza ukuvumelanisa nokubuyisela ukufinyelela kwakho.
- Ngaphandle kwe-Google noma i-Apple, **ikhodi yakho yokubuyisela iyona kuphela indlela yokubuyela ku-akhawunti yakho**. Uma uyilahla, asikwazi ukubuyisela ukufinyelela kwakho.
- I-akhawunti engenayo i-Google noma i-Apple **iyasuswa, kanye nedatha yayo, ngemva kwezinsuku ezingu-30 ingasetshenziswa**: lapho ingekho neyodwa yamadivayisi ayo evumelanisa izinsuku ezingu-30. Ikhodi yayo yokubuyisela iyayeka ukusebenza ngaleso sikhathi. Xhuma i-Google noma i-Apple ukuze uyigcine.
- Unomthwalo walokho okwenziwa ngekhodi yakho nangama-akhawunti owaxhumayo. Ungabelani ngekhodi.
- Sebenzisa igama iqembu lakho elilaziyo, futhi ungazenzi omunye umuntu.

## Lokho okwengezayo {#content}

Izindleko, amazwana, izithombe nokhiye obengezayo kuhlala kungokwakho. Ukuze i-app isebenze, usinika ilayisensi yamahhala, yomhlaba wonke, engeyona eyodwa yokugcina, ukukopisha, ukuvumelanisa nokubonisa lokho okuqukethwe kubantu abasemaqenjini akho nakunoma ubani onelinki oyabelana ngayo, uma nje kusekhona kusevisi.

Engeza kuphela abantu, izithombe nolwazi onelungelo lokwabelana ngalo. Wonke umuntu oseqenjini ubona lokho okwengezwa kulo, futhi noma ubani onelinki oyabelana ngayo ubona lokho ekuvulayo ([Inqubomgomo yobumfihlo](/privacy#sees)). Yabelana ngelinki yesindleko ngokuthi “Noma ubani onelinki” kuphela nabantu abangasibona leso sindleko.

## Ukusebenzisa okwamukelekayo {#rules}

Ungayisebenziseli i-app:

- ukukhwabanisa, ukushushumbisa imali noma yini engekho emthethweni;
- ukuhlukumeza, ukusongela noma ukudalula noma ubani;
- ukuthumela ugaxekile ngezimemo noma ngezaziso;
- ukuthumela okuqukethwe okungekho emthethweni, okuhlambalazayo noma okwephula amalungelo abanye abantu;
- ukuzama ukufinyelela amaqembu angewona akho, ukweqa ukuphepha noma ukugcwalisa ngokweqile isevisi;
- ukuqoqa idatha yabanye abantu ngezindlela ezizenzakalelayo.

## Amarisidi nezibalo {#receipts}

Isifundi samarisidi sisebenzisa ubuhlakani bokwenziwa futhi singase siphazame into, inani noma isamba. I-app ibonisa lokho ekufundile ukuze ukuhlole ngaphambi kokulondoloza, futhi uma sesilondoloziwe sifana nanoma isiphi isindleko esengezwe ngesandla.

Amanani abalwa ngokwalokho iqembu elikwengezayo nokuhlukanisa okukhethile. Hlola izinombolo ngaphambi kokukhokha noma ukucela othile ukuthi akhokhe.

## Ukutholakala {#availability}

I-app isebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi futhi ivumelanisa uma kunokuxhumana, okuncike kunethiwekhi yakho namaseva ethu. Isevisi ingase ingatholakali ngenxa yokulungiswa noma yokwehluleka okungaphezu kwamandla ethu.

Singase sishintshe, sengeze noma sisuse izici. Uma ushintsho luthinta into osuvele uyisebenzisa, siyakutshela ku-app kuqala.

## Intengo {#price}

Uma kukhona izici ezikhokhelwayo, intengo nemibandela kuboniswa ngaphambi kwanoma iyiphi inkokhiso, futhi ukuthenga kulandela imithetho ye-Google Play ne-App Store, kuhlanganise nokukhansela nokubuyiselwa kwemali.

## Uphawu nobunikazi {#brand}

Amagama athi Blitz Split no-Bora Rachar, isithonjana nokubukeka kwe-app kungokwethu. Le migomo ayikunikezi ilungelo lokukusebenzisa ngaphandle kwe-app.

## Ukuphela {#end}

Ungayeka ukusebenzisa i-app noma nini lapho ufuna futhi usule i-akhawunti yakho ku-Iphrofayela › “Susa i-akhawunti nedatha”. Okwenzekayo kudatha yakho kuku-[Nqubomgomo yobumfihlo](/privacy#keep).

Singase simise noma sivale i-akhawunti ephula le migomo, sikuxwayise kuqala noma nini lapho singakwazi.

## Umthwalo wemfanelo {#liability}

Senza konke esingakwenza ukuze i-app ihlale ilungile futhi ixhunyiwe, kodwa inikezwa njengoba injalo. Ngokuvumela komthetho, asinamthwalo wemfanelo:

- wezivumelwano nezinkokhelo phakathi kwamalungu eqembu;
- wedatha elahlekile kanye nekhodi yokubuyisela, noma kanye nedivayisi ngaphambi kokuba ivumelanise;
- wedatha ye-akhawunti engenayo i-Google noma i-Apple, esuswe ngemva kwezinsuku ezingu-30 ingasetshenziswa;
- wamaphutha edatha abantu abayengezile, noma efundwe kumarisidi yalondolozwa ingahloliwe.

Akukho lapha okunciphisa amalungelo onikezwa wona yiKhodi Yokuvikelwa Kwabathengi yaseBrazil.
