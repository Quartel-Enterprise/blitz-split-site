## What the app does {#service}

Blitz Split helps groups share expenses: it records bills, works out who owes whom how much, and keeps the payments you record.

> **It is not a bank or a payment institution.**
>
> No money goes through the app. Pix, Bitcoin or any other method happens outside it, between you and your banks or wallets. The app only shows the key or address each member saved.

Balances are the result of what the group added. They are not a bill, a credit instrument or proof of debt.

The receipt reader uses artificial intelligence and may get an item, a price or the total wrong. The app shows what it read for you to check before saving, and once saved it is like any bill added by hand.

Amounts are worked out from what the group adds and the split you picked. Check the numbers before paying or asking someone to pay.
